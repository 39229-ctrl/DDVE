# America's Greatest Sports Website

A beautiful, responsive website showcasing the four major professional sports leagues in the United States.

## Featured Sports

### 🏈 National Football League (NFL)
- America's most popular sport by viewership
- Championship: Super Bowl
- Season: September - February
- 32 teams

### 🏀 National Basketball Association (NBA)
- World's premier basketball league with global stars
- Championship: NBA Finals
- Season: October - June
- 30 teams

### ⚾ Major League Baseball (MLB)
- America's pastime with over 150 years of history
- Championship: World Series
- Season: March - October
- 30 teams

### 🏒 National Hockey League (NHL)
- Premier professional ice hockey league
- Championship: Stanley Cup
- Season: October - June
- 32 teams

## Features

- **Responsive Design**: Works perfectly on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices
- **Modern Styling**: Clean, professional design with gradient backgrounds
- **Navigation**: Easy-to-use navigation bar for quick access to each sport
- **Interactive Elements**: Hover effects and smooth transitions
- **Fun Facts**: Interesting information about American sports
- **SEO Optimized**: Proper semantic HTML structure

## How to Use

1. Open `index.html` in your web browser
2. Use the navigation bar to jump to different sports sections
3. Hover over cards to see interactive effects
4. Enjoy learning about America's favorite sports!
